# Warm-up, cool-down and recovery

## Warm-up
Spend five to ten minutes raising your heart rate with easy cardio, then do one or two light sets of your first exercise. Dynamic movements like arm circles, leg swings and bodyweight squats prepare the joints. Long static stretching before lifting is not needed.

## Cool-down
Walk for a few minutes and breathe slowly. Light stretching afterwards is fine if it feels good.

## Soreness
Muscle soreness (DOMS) peaks one to two days after a new or harder session and is normal. Sharp, sudden or joint pain is not normal. Stop and rest.

## Rest days
Rest days are when you adapt. Active rest such as walking is great. Aim for at least one or two full rest days a week.
