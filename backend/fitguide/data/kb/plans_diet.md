# Diet plan approaches

## How a day is built
FitGuide takes your calorie and macro targets, splits the calories across breakfast, lunch, snack and dinner (about 25, 35, 15 and 25 percent), and fills each meal with foods from its database. It scales portions to land within about 5 percent of your target, then checks protein.

## Fat loss
Moderate deficit, high protein, lots of vegetables and fruit, filling carbs such as oats, legumes and potatoes, and fewer fried foods and sugary drinks.

## Muscle gain
Small surplus, protein at every meal, plenty of carbs around training and healthy fats from olive oil and nuts.

## Maintenance and fitness
Eat at maintenance with a balanced plate: about half vegetables and fruit, a quarter protein and a quarter whole carbs.

## Egyptian-friendly meals
Plans use foods like ful, eggs, baladi bread, rice, lentils, grilled chicken, fish, yogurt and salads, because they are common, affordable and easy to log.

## Swaps
Any food can be replaced by one with similar calories and protein. For example 120 g grilled chicken breast is similar in protein to 150 g tuna, 3 eggs or 200 g Greek yogurt.
