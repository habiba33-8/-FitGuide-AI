# Water and hydration

## How much water
A practical starting point is about 30 to 35 ml per kg of body weight per day, plus extra on training days and in hot weather. Fluids from tea, soups and fruit count too. Most healthy people can use thirst plus pale yellow urine as a guide.

## Around exercise
Drink a glass of water in the two hours before training and sip during longer or hot sessions. For sessions under an hour, plain water is enough.

## Caution
Drinking far more water than your thirst asks for gives no benefit. People with kidney or heart conditions should follow their doctor's fluid advice.
