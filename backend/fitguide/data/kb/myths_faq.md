# Myths and frequently asked questions

## Can I burn fat from my belly with crunches?
No. Spot reduction does not work. Overall fat loss comes from a calorie deficit, and abs are trained for strength.

## Is cardio in the morning better for fat loss?
Not by itself. The best time is the one you can keep doing. Total weekly activity and calories matter more.

## Will lifting weights make women bulky?
No. Building a lot of muscle takes years of heavy training and a calorie surplus. Lifting helps women get stronger and keep muscle while losing fat.

## Do I need to eat every 2 to 3 hours?
No. Meal timing matters little compared to total calories and protein.

## Do carbs make you fat?
No. Eating more calories than you use does. Carbs are useful fuel for training.

## Do I need supplements?
No. Food comes first. Creatine monohydrate and protein powder are the best studied and are optional.

## How fast will I see results?
Strength usually improves within a few weeks. Visible body changes often take 8 to 12 weeks of consistency.
