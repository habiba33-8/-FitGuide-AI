# Portion sizes

## Hand guide
A palm of cooked meat or fish is about 100 to 120 g and 25 to 30 g protein. A fist of cooked rice or pasta is about 150 g. A cupped hand of nuts is about 28 g. A thumb of oil or butter is about 1 tablespoon (10 to 14 g).

## Common household measures
One cup of cooked rice is about 180 g. One tablespoon of olive oil is about 14 g and 120 kcal. One egg is about 50 g. One slice of bread is about 30 g.

## Why portions matter
For photos and quick logs the amount is the biggest source of error, not the type of food. Oils, sauces and fried foods hide calories. When in doubt, use a kitchen scale for a few days to train your eye.
