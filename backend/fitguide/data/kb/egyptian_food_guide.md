# Egyptian food guide for fitness goals

## Koshari
A plate (about 350 g) has roughly 500 kcal, mostly carbs from rice, pasta and lentils. For a lighter plate use a smaller portion of pasta and rice, more lentils and tomato sauce, and less fried onion.

## Ful medames
Ful is a strong breakfast: about 110 kcal per 100 g with around 7 g protein. Add lemon and a little olive oil instead of lots of oil. A plate with baladi bread is filling and cheap.

## Ta'ameya
Ta'ameya is fried, so it is high in fat: about 330 kcal per 100 g. Baking or air frying lowers the fat. Two or three pieces are a reasonable portion.

## Baladi bread
One loaf of baladi bread is about 230 kcal. Count it when you log a meal, since bread adds up quickly.

## Grilled and stewed dishes
Grilled chicken, kofta, fish and liver are good protein sources. Molokhia, okra (bamia) and mixed vegetable stews are lighter when cooked with little oil. Ask for less ghee or oil.

## Sweets
Basbousa, kahk and om ali are high in sugar and fat. Enjoy a small piece and log it. Dates are a good sweet alternative in small amounts.

## Ideas for a high-protein Egyptian day
Breakfast: boiled eggs, ful and baladi bread with tomato. Lunch: grilled chicken with rice and salad. Snack: Greek yogurt or cottage cheese (areesh) with cucumber. Dinner: lentil soup and tuna salad.
