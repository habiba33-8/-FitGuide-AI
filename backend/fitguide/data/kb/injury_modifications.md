# Injury and discomfort modifications

This is general guidance, not medical advice. For pain that is sharp, getting worse, or lasts more than a few days, see a doctor or physiotherapist.

## Knee discomfort
Use box squats to a comfortable depth, leg press with a smaller range, glute bridges and hamstring work. Avoid deep lunges and jumping until it settles.

## Lower back discomfort
Keep a neutral spine and avoid heavy hinging and sit-ups at first. Choose glute bridges, dead bugs, planks, and machine rows. Walking helps.

## Shoulder discomfort
Avoid overhead pressing and deep dips. Use neutral-grip dumbbell presses, landmine presses, face pulls and band pull-aparts.

## Wrist discomfort
Use dumbbells or handles with a neutral grip, push-ups on fists or handles, and wrist wraps if helpful.

## Returning after a break
Start with about half your old volume and weight and build over two to three weeks.
