# Fat loss basics

## The core idea
Fat loss needs a sustained calorie deficit. A moderate deficit of about 15 to 20 percent below maintenance is easier to sustain than a crash diet and protects muscle.

## Pace
A safe pace is about 0.5 to 1 percent of body weight per week. Faster loss raises the risk of muscle loss, fatigue and rebound eating.

## What helps
Keep protein high, lift weights two to four times a week to keep muscle, walk daily, sleep seven to nine hours and eat mostly whole foods. Weigh yourself under the same conditions and look at the weekly average.

## Plateaus
If weight has not moved for two to three weeks while you follow the plan closely, reduce calories by about 100 to 150 kcal or add 2000 to 3000 steps a day. Do not cut calories below the safe minimum.

## Spot reduction
You cannot choose where fat comes off. Training a muscle builds that muscle but does not burn fat from the area above it.
