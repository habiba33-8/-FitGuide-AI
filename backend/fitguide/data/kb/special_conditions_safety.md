# Special conditions and when to see a doctor

FitGuide gives general wellness information. It does not diagnose or treat any condition.

## Talk to a doctor first if you have
Diabetes or prediabetes, high blood pressure or heart disease, kidney disease, a history of eating disorders, pregnancy or breastfeeding, recent surgery, or any chest pain, fainting or unusual breathlessness with exercise.

## Under 18
Focus on skills, strength and enjoying movement. Do not diet to lose weight without a doctor's or dietitian's guidance.

## Eating disorders
If tracking food makes you anxious or you feel guilty after eating, stop tracking and talk to a professional. Calorie counting can be harmful in this situation.

## Stop exercise if
You feel chest pain, dizziness, severe shortness of breath, or sharp joint pain. Seek medical help if it does not pass quickly.
