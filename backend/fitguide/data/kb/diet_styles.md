# Diet styles and how FitGuide calculates them

## Balanced
About 25 percent of calories from fat, protein from body weight (1.6 to 2.0 g per kg) and the rest from carbohydrates. Works for most goals.

## High protein
Same as balanced but protein is raised by about 0.2 g per kg. Helpful for fat loss and muscle gain, and for staying full. Not appropriate for people with kidney disease.

## Low carb
Fat rises to about 40 percent of calories and carbs fall. Protein stays the same. It is one way to cut calories, not a requirement for fat loss. People with diabetes should ask a doctor before trying it.

## Vegetarian
Protein comes from eggs, dairy, legumes (ful, lentils, chickpeas), tofu and nuts. Combine legumes with grains across the day. Pay attention to iron and vitamin B12.

## Mediterranean pattern
Vegetables, fruit, legumes, whole grains, fish, olive oil and nuts, with less processed meat and sweets. A pattern, not a calorie target. It fits the other styles.

## Intermittent fasting
Eating inside a window such as 8 hours does not burn more fat by itself. It helps some people eat less. The total calories and protein still decide the outcome.
