# Energy and calories

## What a calorie target is
Your body burns energy all day. BMR (basal metabolic rate) is the energy you burn at complete rest. TDEE (total daily energy expenditure) is BMR multiplied by an activity factor. FitGuide estimates BMR with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation: 10 x weight in kg + 6.25 x height in cm - 5 x age, then +5 for men or -161 for women.

## Activity factors
Sedentary 1.2, lightly active 1.375, moderately active 1.55, very active 1.725. People usually overestimate their activity, so start lower and adjust using real weight trends over two to three weeks.

## Goal adjustments
For fat loss, eat about 15 to 20 percent below TDEE. For muscle gain, eat about 5 to 10 percent above TDEE. For maintenance, eat at TDEE. Never go below about 1200 kcal for women or 1500 kcal for men without medical supervision.

## Estimates, not exact numbers
Every calorie number is an estimate. Food labels can be off by 10 to 20 percent and home-cooked meals vary a lot. Use calorie targets as a guide and judge progress by the weekly trend of body weight, energy and performance.
