# Muscle gain basics

## What builds muscle
Three things matter most: training a muscle with enough hard sets, enough protein and a small calorie surplus, and enough sleep to recover.

## Training volume
A useful range is about 10 to 20 hard sets per muscle group per week, spread over two sessions. Beginners often progress with the low end. A hard set is stopped about one to three reps before failure.

## Rep ranges
Muscle can grow across a wide range of reps, roughly 5 to 30, as long as sets are close to failure. Use 6 to 12 reps for most exercises and 12 to 20 for isolation work and machines.

## Surplus
Eat about 5 to 10 percent above maintenance. Gaining about 0.25 to 0.5 percent of body weight per week keeps fat gain small.

## Expectations
Beginners can gain muscle faster. After the first year gains slow down. Be patient and track strength in your log.
