# Training and eating during Ramadan

## Training
Many people train lightly before iftar or one to two hours after iftar. Reduce volume by about 20 to 30 percent and focus on keeping strength, not on breaking records.

## Food and water
Break the fast with water and dates, then a balanced meal with protein and vegetables. Spread water between iftar and suhoor. At suhoor eat protein, slow carbs such as oats or ful, and fluids.

## Sleep
Protect sleep as much as possible. A short nap can help.
