# Tracking progress

## What to track
Body weight (weekly average), a waist measurement every two weeks, training logs (weights and reps), photos once a month and how you feel.

## How to read the scale
Weight changes day to day by one to two kg from water and food. Use the weekly average and look at the trend over three or four weeks.

## Adjusting the plan
FitGuide renews your plan every week. If you complete most workouts and the trend matches your goal, it adds a small progression. If you miss many sessions, it keeps the load and makes the week simpler. If weight moves much faster or slower than the safe pace, it adjusts calories a little.
