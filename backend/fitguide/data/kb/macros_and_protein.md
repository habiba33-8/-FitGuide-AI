# Macronutrients and protein

## Protein
Protein supports muscle repair and keeps you full. A common evidence-based range for people who train is 1.6 to 2.2 grams per kg of body weight per day. Spread it over three or four meals. Good sources: chicken, fish, eggs, yogurt, cottage cheese, lentils, beans, ful, tofu, milk.

## Carbohydrates
Carbs fuel training. Prefer whole sources such as oats, brown rice, potatoes, sweet potato, fruit and legumes, and keep sweets and sugary drinks occasional. Carbs have 4 kcal per gram.

## Fat
Fat supports hormones and absorbing vitamins. Aim for about 20 to 35 percent of calories. Prefer olive oil, nuts, seeds, avocado and fish. Fat has 9 kcal per gram.

## Calories from macros
Protein 4 kcal per gram, carbohydrate 4 kcal per gram, fat 9 kcal per gram. FitGuide sets protein first from body weight, fat as a share of calories, and fills the rest with carbohydrates.

## Fiber
Aim for 25 to 35 grams of fiber a day from vegetables, fruit, legumes and whole grains. It helps fullness and digestion.
