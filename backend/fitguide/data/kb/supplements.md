# Supplements (general information)

Supplements are optional and cannot replace food, sleep and training.

## Protein powder
A convenient way to reach a protein target. Whey is common. Choose one tested by a third party.

## Creatine monohydrate
One of the best studied supplements for strength. A typical amount is 3 to 5 g a day. People with kidney disease should ask a doctor first.

## Caffeine
Can improve performance in moderate amounts. Avoid it late in the day and if it makes you anxious.

## Caution
Do not use fat burners or products that promise fast results. Ask a doctor or pharmacist before taking anything if you are pregnant, take medicine or have a medical condition. FitGuide does not give doses for medical use.
